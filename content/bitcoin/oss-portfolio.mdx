Your portfolio is evidence: repositories, PRs, writeups, and demos. Hiring managers and OSS maintainers trust artifacts over adjectives.

## What to include

- Mini wallet / explorer / processor links
- 2, 3 merged (or deeply reviewed) PRs
- A diagram of architecture for one project
- Regtest demo script or short recording
