Reviewing is how Bitcoin stays safe. You will learn more from careful review than from rushing another feature PR.

Use Pull's **Review Club** picks on this lesson, or browse [open issues](/issues) filtered for Bitcoin tracks.

## What to check

1. Does it solve the claimed problem?
2. Are there tests?
3. Are edge cases handled?
4. Is the diff minimal?

    PR[Open PR] --> Read[Read description]
    Read --> Diff[Inspect diff]
    Diff --> Test[Run tests]

<Callout type="info" title="Be precise">
  Prefer “This breaks reorg handling because X” over “Hmm not sure.”
