**LND** is a widely deployed Lightning implementation with gRPC/REST APIs and macaroon-based auth. Many apps integrate here first.

## Why this matters

Production integrations live or die on auth scoping, idempotent payment calls, and careful macaroon storage.

## Analogy

Macaroons are VIP wristbands with permissions printed on them. Anyone holding an admin wristband is you, guard them like passwords.

<Mermaid caption="Apps authenticate to LND; LND talks to Bitcoin and peers." chart="flowchart LR
    App[Your app] --> Mac[Macaroon + TLS]
    Mac --> LND[LND]
    LND --> Bitcoind[bitcoind]
    LND --> Peers[LN peers]" />

```bash
lncli getinfo
lncli addinvoice --amt 1000
lncli payinvoice <bolt11>
```

<Callout type="danger" title="Macaroon hygiene">
  Do not commit macaroons. Use readonly macaroons for UIs that only display data.
</Callout>
