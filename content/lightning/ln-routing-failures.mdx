Most Lightning payments fail at least once before succeeding. Good software treats failure as information, not as a dead end.

## Why this matters

Naive “try once and show error” UX makes Lightning feel broken. Smart retries with fee budgets make it feel instant.

## Failure classes

- Temporary channel imbalance / unknown liquidity
- Expiry too tight
- Fee insufficient
- Permanent: unknown peer, invalid invoice, exhausted expiry

## Hands-on habit

Every protocol idea should be paired with one local experiment:

1. Reproduce the happy path on regtest (or Polar for Lightning)
2. Break it on purpose (wrong fee, expired invoice, offline peer)
3. Write down what error you saw and which layer produced it (wallet, node, mempool, peer)

That habit turns reading into builder instinct.
