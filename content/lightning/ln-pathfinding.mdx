**Pathfinding** searches the gossip graph (plus hints) for a path that can deliver an amount under fee and timelock constraints, even with incomplete liquidity knowledge.

## Why this matters

Dijkstra-with-fees is only the start. Production routers track historical failures and prefer reliable edges.

## Analogy

GPS with imperfect live traffic. You pick a route, hit a jam (insufficient balance), then reroute using what you learned.

<Mermaid caption="Search, attempt, learn, retry. Liquidity is uncertain." chart="flowchart TD

## Hands-on habit

Every protocol idea should be paired with one local experiment:

1. Reproduce the happy path on regtest (or Polar for Lightning)
2. Break it on purpose (wrong fee, expired invoice, offline peer)
3. Write down what error you saw and which layer produced it (wallet, node, mempool, peer)

That habit turns reading into builder instinct.
