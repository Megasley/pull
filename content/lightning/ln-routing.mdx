Lightning uses **source-based routing**: the payer (usually) constructs a path across the public graph plus hints, then onion-encrypts hop instructions.

## Why this matters

Routing success rates define UX. Fee-aware, reliability-aware pathfinding separates toy demos from production wallets.

## Analogy

You plan a road trip with toll costs and bridge weight limits. If a bridge is “full” (insufficient balance), you detour, often without knowing the exact traffic until you try.

## Common mistakes

- Optimizing only for lowest fee and ignoring success probability
- Ignoring max HTLC / capacity constraints
- Forgetting private channel hints from invoices
