A **watchtower** monitors the blockchain for revoked commitment broadcasts on your behalf and can publish justice transactions if you are offline.

Complete **Step 1 // Required reading** in the study plan above, then continue here. This closes **Implementation**.

## Step 2 — Core idea: someone watches when you sleep

Phones sleep. Laptops close. Lightning’s penalty model needs someone watching. Towers extend security to intermittent devices.

**Analogy:** A neighborhood security camera that only unlocks the evidence locker if a specific break-in pattern appears — without learning your daily life (ideally).
